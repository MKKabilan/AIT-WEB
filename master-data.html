Master Data

Master data is the backbone of every laboratory informatics system. Our master data services ensure your product, material, test, and specification data is accurately structured, consistently configured, and rigorously verified to support compliant and efficient operations.

OVERVIEW

Build a Solid Foundation with Accurate Master Data

Master data forms the structural backbone of every laboratory informatics platform, governing how products, materials, tests, specifications, and methods are defined, linked, and consumed across the entire system. When master data is incomplete, inconsistent, or incorrectly configured, downstream processes such as sample login, testing, result entry, reporting, and release become error-prone and audit-risky. At AssureInfo Technologies, we treat master data as a strategic asset and help organizations design, configure, and validate it with precision.

Our consultants bring deep domain expertise across LIMS, ELN, LES, and related laboratory platforms used in regulated industries. We follow a structured methodology that begins with a comprehensive assessment of your current master data landscape, identifies gaps and redundancies, and defines a clean, scalable configuration aligned with your business processes. Every record we configure is documented, traceable, and ready for inspection.

Whether you are implementing a new system, migrating from a legacy platform, or stabilizing an existing environment, our master data services reduce rework, accelerate go-live, and improve the long-term reliability of your laboratory operations. We work collaboratively with your subject matter experts to ensure the configuration reflects real-world workflows while meeting global regulatory and data integrity expectations.

CATEGORY A

What We Offer

A complete suite of master data services designed to structure, configure, and verify the core reference data that powers your laboratory operations.

01

Master Data Assessment

Comprehensive evaluation of your existing master data landscape to identify completeness, consistency, accuracy, and compliance gaps. We deliver a prioritized roadmap that defines what needs to be created, corrected, migrated, or retired before any configuration begins.

02

Product and Material Master Configuration

Structured setup of product and material records including attributes, hierarchies, classifications, specifications, and relationships. Our configuration approach ensures downstream sampling, testing, and reporting workflows function correctly across all product lines and sites.

03

Test and Specification Configuration

Definition of tests, specifications, limits, units, and acceptance criteria that align with your quality protocols and regulatory filings. We ensure specifications are version-controlled, audit-trailed, and ready for stability, release, and in-process testing scenarios.

04

Test Method Configuration With/Without Calculation

Configuration of test methods including formulas, calculations, rounding rules, and result-entry behaviour. We support both simple pass/fail methods and complex multi-step calculations, ensuring results are computed consistently and reproducibly every time.

05

Master Data Verification

Independent verification of configured master data against source specifications, SOPs, and regulatory documents. We confirm that every record, attribute, and relationship is accurate, complete, and traceable before the system goes live or before any change is promoted to production.

VALUE

Key Benefits

Investing in clean, well-structured master data pays dividends across every downstream laboratory process.

Improved Data Accuracy

Consistent, validated reference data eliminates transcription errors, mis-samples, and incorrect results across the entire laboratory workflow.

Regulatory Compliance

Master data aligned with 21 CFR Part 11, EU Annex 11, and ALCOA+ principles stands up to internal audits and external regulatory inspections.

Operational Efficiency

Correctly configured products, materials, and tests streamline sample login, testing, and reporting, reducing manual intervention and rework.

Scalable Foundation

A clean, well-structured master data model scales effortlessly as your product portfolio, sites, and testing scope grow over time.
