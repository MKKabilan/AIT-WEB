OVERVIEW

From Vision to Go-Live, Managed End to End

Implementing a laboratory informatics or enterprise system in a regulated environment is far more than a technical exercise. It is a transformation programme that touches people, processes, data, and compliance. A poorly managed implementation can lead to scope creep, budget overruns, validation failures, and user resistance that erode the value of the investment. At AssureInfo Technologies, we bring a disciplined, methodology-driven approach that keeps every implementation on time, on budget, and audit-ready.

Our implementation framework spans the full project lifecycle, beginning with deep business requirement analysis and gap assessment, progressing through process design and system configuration, and culminating in user acceptance testing, validation, training, and go-live. We work shoulder-to-shoulder with your business and IT stakeholders, translating operational needs into configured functionality that is both compliant and genuinely useful to end users.

What sets our implementations apart is the continuity of care. We do not disappear after go-live. Our post-implementation support ensures the system stabilizes quickly, hyper-care issues are resolved promptly, and your internal teams are confident and self-sufficient. The result is a sustainable deployment that delivers long-term value rather than a one-time project milestone.

CATEGORY B

What We Offer

A complete implementation lifecycle covering every phase from initial discovery through sustained post-go-live operations.

01

Business Requirement Analysis

Structured workshops and interviews to capture functional, technical, and regulatory requirements from business, quality, and IT stakeholders. We document requirements in a clear, testable format that becomes the foundation for design, configuration, and acceptance.

02

GAP Assessment

Comparative analysis of your business requirements against the standard capabilities of the chosen platform. We identify configuration gaps, custom development needs, and process changes, and recommend the most sustainable path forward.

03

Process Flow Design

End-to-end design of to-be business processes that align system capabilities with your operational and quality workflows. We map every process step, role, decision point, and data handoff to ensure the configured system supports real-world operations.

04

System Configuration

Hands-on configuration of the platform based on approved design documents, including security, workflows, master data, integrations, and reporting. Every configuration decision is documented and traceable for validation and audit purposes.

05

User Acceptance Testing (UAT)

Design, execution, and management of UAT scenarios that validate the configured system against business requirements. We coordinate testers, track defects to closure, and produce evidence packs that satisfy both business and quality stakeholders.

06

Validation

Execution of the computer system validation lifecycle in line with GAMP 5 and 21 CFR Part 11, including IQ, OQ, and PQ protocols. We deliver traceable, audit-ready validation documentation that demonstrates the system is fit for its intended use.

07

User Training

Role-based training programmes that combine classroom sessions, hands-on workshops, and reference materials. We train super-users and end-users, ensuring smooth adoption and reducing post-go-live support burden.

08

Go-Live and Post-Implementation Support

Cutover planning, go-live execution, and structured hyper-care support to stabilize the system quickly. We resolve issues promptly, monitor adoption, and transition smoothly into steady-state operations.
