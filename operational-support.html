Operational Support

After go-live, the real work begins. Our operational support services keep your laboratory and enterprise systems running smoothly, evolving with your business, and continuously delivering value through responsive support, structured enhancements, and proactive knowledge transfer.

OVERVIEW

Sustainable Operations Long After Go-Live

The period immediately after go-live is when systems are most vulnerable. New users encounter unfamiliar workflows, configurations need fine-tuning, and operational issues that were not visible in testing begin to surface. Without structured operational support, these early issues can cascade into user frustration, workarounds, data integrity risks, and erosion of the value the implementation was meant to deliver. Mature operational support is therefore not a luxury but a necessity for any regulated laboratory or enterprise system.

At AssureInfo Technologies, we provide multi-layered operational support services that span day-to-day production support, user administration, service request and incident management, change request handling, process and workflow enhancements, system upgrades, and structured knowledge transfer. Our support model is designed to be responsive, compliant, and continuously improving, with clear SLAs, escalation paths, and reporting that give stakeholders confidence in the system's reliability.

We view operational support as a partnership, not a helpdesk. Our consultants get to know your system, your users, and your business context, allowing us to resolve issues faster, recommend improvements proactively, and help your internal teams grow in capability and confidence over time. The goal is simple, a stable, compliant, and continuously improving system environment that supports your operational and regulatory objectives year after year.

What We Offer

A comprehensive operational support portfolio covering every aspect of keeping your systems healthy, compliant, and continuously improving.

01

Production Operational Support

Day-to-day monitoring, troubleshooting, and resolution of production issues across your laboratory and enterprise systems. We provide tiered support with clear escalation paths so the right expertise is applied to every issue, fast.

02

User Administration and Access Management

Management of user accounts, roles, permissions, and access reviews in line with your security and compliance policies. We ensure the right people have the right access at the right time, with full audit trail and segregation of duties.

03

Service Request, Incident, and Change Requests Management

Structured handling of service requests, incident resolution, and change requests using ITIL aligned processes. Every ticket is logged, categorized, prioritized, resolved, and documented to support both operations and audit.

04

Process/Workflow Enhancements

Design and delivery of incremental improvements to configured workflows, forms, reports, and integrations as your business needs evolve. Enhancements are scoped, validated, and released with the same rigour as the original implementation.

05

System Upgrades

Planning and execution of version upgrades, patches, and technology stack updates with minimal business disruption. We manage regression testing, validation, and cutover to ensure upgrades deliver value without introducing risk.

06

Knowledge Transfer and Training

Structured knowledge transfer sessions, documentation, and refresher training to build internal capability and reduce dependency on external support. We grow your super-users into self-sufficient champions of the platform.
