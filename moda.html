Laboratory Quality Management Software

MODA is a Laboratory Quality Management Software platform that streamlines deviations, CAPA, change control, complaints, audits, and document management in a single, audit-trailed environment. OVERVIEW

Streamlined Quality Management in a Single Platform

Quality management in regulated laboratories involves a complex web of interconnected processes including deviation handling, CAPA, change control, complaint management, internal audits, supplier qualifications, document control, and training management. Managing these processes across disconnected spreadsheets, paper forms, and email threads creates significant inefficiencies, data integrity risks, and audit exposure. A unified Laboratory Quality Management Software (LQMS) platform like MODA addresses these challenges by bringing every quality process into a single, controlled, and audit-trailed environment.

At AssureInfo Technologies, we help laboratories and quality organizations implement, configure, validate, and support MODA platforms that genuinely strengthen quality operations. Our consultants understand the underlying quality processes that MODA supports and the regulatory expectations that govern them, ensuring your implementation is not just technically functional but operationally effective and inspection-ready.

Our MODA services span the full lifecycle from requirements and design through configuration, validation, integration, training, and operational support. Whether you are deploying MODA for the first time, expanding its scope to additional quality processes, or stabilizing an existing environment, we bring the quality domain expertise and technical execution needed to deliver a quality management platform that drives continuous improvement and regulatory confidence.

What We Offer

Comprehensive MODA services covering implementation, configuration, validation, and operational support.

01

MODA Implementation

Full lifecycle implementation of MODA including requirements analysis, process design, configuration, UAT, validation, training, and go-live. We tailor the platform to your quality processes while preserving regulatory compliance and audit readiness.

02

Quality Process Configuration

Configuration of deviations, CAPA, change control, complaints, audits, document control, training, and supplier management workflows. We map your quality SOPs into structured, controlled electronic workflows with full audit trail.

03

MODA Validation

Risk-based CSV validation of MODA in line with GAMP 5 and 21 CFR Part 11. We deliver IQ, OQ, and PQ protocols, traceability matrices, and validation summary reports that demonstrate the platform is fit for its intended use.

04

MODA Integration

Integration of MODA with LIMS, ERP, QMS, document management, and training systems. We connect quality processes with operational data to enable root cause analysis, trend detection, and proactive quality management.

05

Document Control & Training Setup

Configuration of document control hierarchies, versioning rules, effective dating, training assignments, and competency tracking. We ensure your controlled documents and training records are managed defensibly and accessibly.

06

MODA Operational Support

Tiered production support, user administration, change management, and continuous improvement. We keep your MODA environment aligned with evolving quality processes and regulatory expectations.

WHERE IT'S USED

Where It's Used

MODA supports a wide range of quality management scenarios across regulated industries.

Deviation Management

Structured capture, investigation, root cause analysis, and corrective action tracking for laboratory and manufacturing deviations.

CAPA Programmes

Corrective and preventive action management with action tracking, effectiveness checks, and closure workflows.

Change Control

Structured change request evaluation, impact assessment, approval, and implementation tracking for GMP changes.

Document Control

Version-controlled SOP, specification, and method management with effective dating, training assignments, and access controls.

Training Management

Role-based training assignment, completion tracking, and competency management for laboratory and quality personnel.

Audit & Inspection Support

Rapid retrieval of quality records, investigations, and approvals in support of internal audits and regulatory inspections.
