Computer System Validation (CSV)

In regulated industries, every GxP computer system must be validated to demonstrate it is fit for its intended use. Our CSV services cover the full validation lifecycle from GxP assessment through validation summary report, aligned with GAMP 5, 21 CFR Part 11, and EU Annex 11.

OVERVIEW

Validation That Stands Up to Inspection

Computer System Validation is a regulatory expectation and a business necessity for any GxP system used in pharmaceutical, biotechnology, healthcare, food, and other regulated environments. Validation demonstrates that a system consistently produces results that meet pre-defined acceptance criteria and that data integrity, security, and audit trail requirements are met throughout the system lifecycle. A weak or incomplete validation effort exposes the organization to observations, warning letters, and operational disruption.

At AssureInfo Technologies, we deliver risk-based, GAMP 5 aligned validation services that are proportionate to the system's complexity, criticality, and intended use. We do not over-document simple systems or under-validate critical ones. Our validation specialists work alongside your business, quality, and IT teams to define the validation strategy, execute the validation deliverables, and produce a complete, audit-ready documentation set that satisfies regulators and internal quality units alike.

Our CSV portfolio spans the full lifecycle from initial GxP assessment and validation master planning through requirement and design specifications, risk assessment, test protocol execution including IQ, OQ, and PQ, traceability matrix development, and validation summary reporting. Every deliverable is built to be defensible, traceable, and reusable across future changes, releases, and revalidations.

CATEGORY E

What We Offer

A complete validation lifecycle deliverable suite covering every document and test required for a defensible, inspection-ready CSV package.

01

GxP Assessment

Initial evaluation of the system to determine its GxP impact, regulatory applicability, and the appropriate validation approach. The GxP assessment is the gating decision that defines the scope, depth, and rigour of all downstream validation activities.

02

Requirement Specification

Documented functional, regulatory, and technical requirements that the system must meet. We write requirement statements that are clear, testable, and traceable, forming the foundation for design, testing, and acceptance.

03

Validation Master Plan

Strategic document defining the validation approach, scope, roles, responsibilities, deliverables, schedule, and acceptance criteria for the system. The VMP aligns stakeholders and governs the entire validation effort.

04

Functional Specification

Detailed description of how the system functions to meet the documented requirements. The functional specification bridges requirements and design and serves as the basis for OQ test protocol development.

05

Risk Assessment

Structured failure mode and effects analysis that identifies potential risks, their causes, and their impact on product quality, data integrity, and patient safety. The risk assessment drives the depth and focus of validation testing.

06

Design Specification

Technical description of the system architecture, configuration, integrations, security, and data model. The design specification ensures the system is built or configured to meet both functional and regulatory expectations.

07

Test Protocols

Pre-approved documents describing the test approach, scope, prerequisites, and responsibilities for each validation phase. Protocols define what will be tested, how, by whom, and what constitutes an acceptable result.

08

Test Scripts (IQ, OQ, and PQ)

Detailed step-by-step test scripts for Installation Qualification, Operational Qualification, and Performance Qualification. Each script includes test steps, expected results, actual results, evidence capture, and reviewer sign-off.

09

Traceability Matrix

Cross-reference document linking every requirement to its corresponding design element, risk, and test script, with the final test result. The traceability matrix demonstrates complete coverage and is a primary inspection artifact.

10

Validation Summary Report

Final report summarizing the entire validation effort, including scope, deliverables completed, test results, deviations, and a clear conclusion that the system is fit for its intended use and approved for release.
