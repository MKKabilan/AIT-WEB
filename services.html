Frequently Asked Questions

Services FAQ

Answers to common questions about our laboratory informatics services, validation, integration, and support offerings.

What is LIMS and how does AssureInfo implement it?
A Laboratory Information Management System (LIMS) manages samples, instruments, workflows, results, and reports in a regulated laboratory. AssureInfo implements LIMS through a structured methodology — business requirement analysis, gap assessment, process flow design, system configuration, UAT, validation, user training, and go-live support.
How long does a typical LIMS implementation take?
Implementation timelines depend on scope, number of labs, integrations, and validation requirements. A focused single-site LIMS deployment typically takes 3–6 months, while enterprise multi-site rollouts with ERP/MES integration can span 9–18 months. We provide a detailed project plan after the discovery and analysis phases.
What is Computer System Validation (CSV) and why is it required?
CSV is the documented process of assuring that a computerized system consistently produces results meeting predetermined specifications and quality attributes. It is required for GxP-regulated systems in pharma, biotech, healthcare, and food industries to demonstrate regulatory compliance during audits and inspections.
Which regulatory standards does AssureInfo comply with?
Our validation services align with GAMP 5, 21 CFR Part 11, EU Annex 11, ALCOA+ data integrity principles, and applicable GxP regulations. We build traceability matrices, risk assessments, and validation summary reports that stand up to FDA, EMA, and other regulatory inspections.
Can you integrate LIMS with our existing ERP systems?
Yes. We have expertise integrating LIMS with SAP ERP, SAP S/4HANA, MES, TrackWise, Documentum, and other enterprise applications. We use REST APIs, web services, file-based, and middleware-based integration patterns to ensure reliable, real-time data exchange with full traceability.
What laboratory instruments can be integrated with LIMS?
We support integration with balances, chromatographs (Empower, Chromeleon), spectrophotometers, dissolution testers, and other analytical instruments via Serial Port, TCP/IP, OPC, file-based transfer, and REST API. We also integrate with chromatography data systems for automated result capture.
What does digital transformation in a laboratory involve?
Digital transformation includes process and workflow automation, electronic logbooks, ELN, paperless lab initiatives, digital documentation, dashboards, business intelligence, data analytics, KPI reporting, Power BI integration, and building AI-ready digital infrastructure for future intelligent automation.
Do you provide post-implementation support?
Yes. We provide long-term managed services including production operational support, user administration, access management, service request and incident management, change requests, process/workflow enhancements, system upgrades, knowledge transfer, and end user training.
What are your support service levels and response times?
We offer tiered SLAs based on incident severity — Critical (L1) issues typically receive response within 30 minutes and resolution targets within 4 hours, while standard service requests are handled within defined business-hour windows. We provide 24×7 support coverage for mission-critical regulated environments.
How do you ensure data integrity in laboratory systems?
We apply ALCOA+ principles — Attributable, Legible, Contemporaneous, Original, Accurate, plus Complete, Consistent, Enduring, and Available. Our implementations include audit trails, electronic signatures, role-based access, version control, and validation deliverables that demonstrate data integrity throughout the data lifecycle.
Can you help with master data migration from legacy systems?
Yes. Master data migration is a core part of our Master Data Management service. We assess legacy data, design the target master data model (products, materials, tests, specifications, methods, formulas, sample types, instruments, user roles), perform data cleansing, verification, quality review, and execute controlled migration with full traceability and validation support.
Do you offer training for end users and administrators?
Yes. We deliver role-based training programs for end users, super users, lab managers, QA, and system administrators. Training includes standard operating procedures, hands-on lab workflows, system administration, troubleshooting, and knowledge transfer sessions with comprehensive documentation for long-term self-sufficiency.
